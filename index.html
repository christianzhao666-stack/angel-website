02 — Process

Ten steps. Every piano.

From incoming inspection to container loading — quality checks at every stage, and only approved instruments proceed to shipping.

  1. Incoming inspection

    Pianos arrive at our central warehouse. Every instrument undergoes professional assessment; only pianos suitable for reconditioning to near-new condition enter the workshop.

  2. Deep cleaning

    Interior and exterior surfaces are thoroughly cleaned to remove years of dust, stains, and residue.

  3. Case refinishing & mirror polishing

    Fine sanding and polishing achieve a mirror finish, removing scratches and blemishes.

  4. Keytop polishing & replacement

    Keytops are polished to an ivory-smooth feel; cracked or damaged surfaces are replaced with matched materials.

  5. Action component replacement

    Technicians replace and adjust internal action components — every part must meet 0.01 mm tolerances.

  6. Complete piano rebuild

    For clients requiring a full upgrade, strings, hammers, and other core components are rebuilt and precisely adjusted.

  7. Action regulation & keybed setup

    Key weight, height, and dip are measured and regulated to factory specification.

  8. Regulation, voicing & tuning

    Note by note, for even tone colour and balance across the full range, followed by precision tuning.

  9. Pre-shipment final inspection

    All components are verified. Any defect requires rework; only approved instruments proceed to shipping.

  10. Container loading & export

    Refurbished pianos are professionally packed into ocean containers and shipped from the Port of Dalian to partners worldwide.

04 — Since 1861 · A Treaty Port

Our City — Yingkou

Angel Musical is based in Yingkou, Liaoning, where the Liao River meets Liaodong Bay. Under the Treaty of Tianjin (1858), the river port of Newchwang was designated for foreign trade; because the river there had silted, the port opened downstream at Yingkou in 1861 — the first treaty port in Northeast China. Western traders kept the treaty's name, Newchwang.

In 1864 the Newchwang Customs House opened here, the first customs office in the region. For decades the city was a principal outlet for the trade of Manchuria; consulates, trading houses, banks and churches followed, and buildings from that era still stand along the old riverfront.

Today the Port of Yingkou, with its deep-water Bayuquan harbour, is one of the largest in Northeast China, and Dalian lies about 200 km to the south. We work from a city that has traded with the world for more than 160 years.
